Conceptual vision · Casa La Piedad, Isla Verde

Isla Verde, Puerto Rico

A home shaped by light, garden and the sea.

Casa La Piedad Residences is envisioned as a home for adults 65 and over who value a residential setting and a lively daily social life, with assistance that grows as their needs change. A serene community for 150 residents, where independence is honored, every day feels unhurried, and belonging comes naturally.

Luminous white volumes gather around a living courtyard, opening to shade, breeze and the quiet rhythm of the coast.

Architecture

Sculptural calm.

Form

Clean geometry, softened by nature.

Rounded white forms, warm wood screens and expansive glass create a contemporary tropical language: elegant, sheltering and open to the island air.

Arrival

A threshold that feels like welcome.

Still water, dappled shade and a generous wooden door mark the moment of arriving home.

The Courtyard

  1. At the heart, a garden.

  2. Light that moves with the day.

  3. Inside and outside, as one.

Garden & Wellness

A garden to share. A place to move.

Shaded pergolas, long tables and gentle paths invite residents to gather, talk and linger, while a light-filled wellness pavilion opens onto the garden for movement, balance and calm.

“Dignity, independence and connection, held within a garden by the sea.”

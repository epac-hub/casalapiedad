Conceptual vision · Casa La Piedad, Isla Verde

Isla Verde, Puerto Rico

A home shaped by light, garden and the sea.

Casa La Piedad Residences is envisioned as a home for adults 65 and over who value a residential setting and a lively daily social life, with assistance that grows as their needs change. A serene community for 150 residents, where independence is honored, every day feels unhurried, and belonging comes naturally.

Luminous white volumes gather around a living courtyard, opening to shade, breeze and the quiet rhythm of the coast.

Architecture

Sculptural calm.

Form

Clean geometry, softened by nature.

Rounded white forms, warm wood screens and expansive glass create a contemporary tropical language: elegant, sheltering and open to the island air.

Arrival

A threshold that feels like welcome.

Still water, dappled shade and a generous wooden door mark the moment of arriving home.

The Courtyard

  1. At the heart, a garden.

  2. Light that moves with the day.

  3. Inside and outside, as one.

Garden & Wellness

A garden to share. A place to move.

Shaded pergolas, long tables and gentle paths invite residents to gather, talk and linger, while a light-filled wellness pavilion opens onto the garden for movement, balance and calm.

Dining

A table worth gathering for.

Chef-led menus served in a warm, light-filled dining room that opens to the garden terrace: unhurried breakfasts, long lunches and candlelit dinners among friends.

Wellness Bar

Nourishment, personally guided.

Fresh juices, smoothies and light plates, with a nutritionist who helps each resident shape a personal meal plan for energy, balance and enjoyment.

Salon & Barber

Feeling your best, close to home.

A calm, private salon and barbershop for cuts, color, styling, shaves and manicures, by appointment and only steps from home.

Pool, Golf & Play

Days with room to enjoy.

Set inland, sheltered by the residences and away from the shoreline: a quiet pool terrace, putting greens for golf and courts for tennis and pickleball.

Cinema

Films, in good company.

A minimal screening room with wide, comfortable armchairs, step-free aisles and warm acoustics, made for afternoon matinées and evening premieres among friends.

Stage

Music, theatre and conversation.

The same hall opens to live performance: concerts, plays, talks and celebrations, presented for residents and their families.

“Dignity, independence and connection, held within a garden near the sea.”
